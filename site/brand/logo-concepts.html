Unbox Learning: logo concepts

Ten marks, each built on one idea from the product. Each is shown on light and dark backgrounds, beside the wordmark, at favicon size (16, 32 and 48 px) and as an app icon. The wordmark is set lowercase in Bricolage Grotesque, the site's heading font.

Recommended
Concept 1

Spark Box

An open box seen from above. Its four flaps fan out into a spark: the moment knowledge is unboxed.

  • Four flaps, four colours: read, hear, try, ask
  • The pinwheel turn suggests motion and play
  • The dot in the middle is the idea itself
  • Clearest at 16 px, and easy to animate (flaps opening)

Prism Box

A box that works like a prism: one plain line goes in, four coloured rays come out. One source, many ways to understand it.

  • Says "one concept, more than one way in" in a single image
  • Shows the transformation: in on the left, out on the right
  • Keeps the isometric box from the current logo
  • The rays could animate, each lighting up in turn
