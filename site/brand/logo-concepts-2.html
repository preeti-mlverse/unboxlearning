Unbox Learning: logo concepts, round 2

Ten sample logos, all built from the box, one for each idea you shared. All ten are side by side first; below, each is shown on light and dark, with the wordmark, at favicon sizes and as an app icon.

The Unfolding Box

A box whose panels open outward into four learning modes: see it, hear it, try it, remember it.

  • Each flap carries its mode: eye, voice, pointer, memory loop
  • Tells the product story in one mark
  • Icons get too small at 16 px; use the plain flaps there
