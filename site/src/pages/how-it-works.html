Questions

How it works, in detail.

How long does it take to unbox a source?

Understanding the material takes about a minute. The outline takes one or two more. A 20-minute learning experience is generated in two to five minutes.

Who decides what gets taught?

You do. UnboxEd proposes goals and an outline; you choose the learner, pick or write the goal, include or leave out sections, and rename, reorder, add or remove modules before anything is generated.

Does it make things up?

Every card is written from the passages of your material that support it and then checked against those passages. Anything that can't be traced back is flagged for you to review.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Spanish, French, German and Arabic today, with more to come.

Who owns what I create?

You do. Your material and the learning made from it are yours, and we don't use your content to train AI models.
