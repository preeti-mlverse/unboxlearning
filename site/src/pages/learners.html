Five minutes at a time

What learning with UnboxEd feels like.

1

Start small

A five-minute module, one idea per card, with a picture or diagram for every idea.

2

Try it

A quick game every card or two: sort it, order it, match it, or move a slider and see what happens.

3

Ask your tutor

Stuck? Ask by typing or speaking. You get a hint first, so you still get to work it out.

4

Refresh this

The ideas come back in your memory deck just before you'd forget them. Keep your streak going.
