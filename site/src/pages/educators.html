Questions educators ask

Before you try it.

Will it follow my textbook?

Yes. UnboxEd works only from the material you give it, and every card is checked against that material before learners see it.

Can I differentiate for different learners?

Set the age, level, language and time for each version. The same chapter can become a simpler visual journey for one group and a challenge set for another.

Can I change what it generates?

Everything is editable: rewrite any card yourself, ask the AI to rewrite it with an instruction such as "simpler words" or "use a local example", or regenerate the picture.

How do learners get it?

Share one link. Learners open it on any phone or laptop, with nothing to install.
