LearnerWait, so a tree's wood comes from the soil, right?

Common misconception spotted

TutorLots of people think so. In the 1640s, Jan van Helmont tested it: he grew a willow in a pot for five years. The tree gained about 74 kg. The soil lost about 57 g. So where did the tree's weight come from?
