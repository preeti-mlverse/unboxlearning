Questions

About plans and pilots.

What does a pilot include?

We build your first course with you from your own material, help you share it with a group of learners, and review the results together. There's no charge during the pilot.

What happens after the pilot?

You choose a plan at the founding price we agree with you, or stop. Your courses stay yours either way.

Is my material safe?

Your material and courses are visible only to you and the people you share them with. We don't use your content to train AI models. See our privacy policy.

Do learners pay?

No. Learners never pay to take a course shared with them.
