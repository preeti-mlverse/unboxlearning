Most of what people need to learn is already written down.

It's in textbooks, manuals, policies and slide decks. The problem is that reading isn't learning. We built Unbox Learning to unbox that material into something people practise, remember and use.

Why we exist

Good courses take weeks of an expert's time to design. So most material never becomes a course at all: it stays a PDF that people skim once and forget.

Unbox Learning does the reading, the planning and the first draft, so a teacher or trainer can turn their own material into an interactive course in an afternoon, and spend their time on the parts that need a person.

What we believe

We build on a few ideas from learning science that are well established and rarely used:

  • Retrieval beats re-reading. Answering a question about an idea fixes it in memory far better than reading it again.
  • Spacing beats cramming. Coming back to an idea just as it starts to fade makes it last.
  • Pictures and words together are easier to understand than either alone.
  • One idea at a time. Short cards and short modules keep attention where it's needed.
  • Hints before answers. A tutor that gives away the answer takes away the learning.

Faithful to the source

Everything the engine writes is traced back to your material and checked against it. If it can't be traced, it's flagged.
