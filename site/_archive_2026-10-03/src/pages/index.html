What makes it different

Built for understanding, not scrolling.

Picture-first cards

Every idea comes with an illustration, a diagram or a figure from your source. One idea per card, never a wall of text.

Mira, the AI tutor

Mira answers only from your material and gives hints before answers, so the learner still does the thinking.

Memory deck

Flashcards come back on a schedule timed to your memory. A few minutes a day beats a night of cramming.

Eight quick games, one every couple of cards

They test whether you understood, not whether you remember the wording.

XP, streaks, chapter rings

Small wins every day. Learners see XP, their streak and how much of each module they have mastered.

Checked against your source

Every card is fact-checked against the material it came from, and anything doubtful is flagged for you before a learner sees it.
