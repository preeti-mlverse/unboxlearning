Home / Features

Everything a course needs, made from what you already have.

For learners, short visual lessons that feel like play. For the people who make courses, a builder that does the heavy lifting and leaves you the final say.

For learners

Five minutes at a time, and it sticks.

Picture-first micro-lessons

Modules of five to seven minutes. One idea per card, each with an illustration, a diagram drawn from the content, or a figure from your source.

Eight quick games

True or false, sort it, put in order, match up, odd one out, fill the gap, quick check and a written "your turn" task. A game every one or two cards.

Feedback that explains

Every answer, right or wrong, comes with the reason. Written answers get specific feedback and a stronger version in the learner's own words.

Mira, the AI tutor

Ask by typing or speaking, and hear the answer read aloud. Mira answers only from the course material and gives hints before answers.

Listen to any card

Every teaching card has narration, so learners can listen while they look at the picture.

Memory deck

Flashcards from every module come back on a schedule timed to memory, plus a time-attack round for quick revision.

XP, streaks and mastery

Learners earn XP, keep a daily streak and see how much of each module they have mastered. Modules unlock as they go.

Ten languages

English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Spanish, French, German and Arabic.

Works on any phone

Runs in the browser on phones, tablets and laptops. Nothing to install.

For course creators

A builder that shows its work.

Add anything

PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, e-books, web pages, whole docs sites, YouTube, audio and video, images and pasted text, mixed in one course.

11 kinds of source

See what the engine understood

Before anything is written you see the subject, the kind of material, the mix of knowledge and how each part will be taught.

content-aware teaching

Suggested goals

Three to six goals the material can genuinely support, from "understand it" to "prepare for an exam". Pick one or write your own.

Edit the outline first

Rename, reorder, add or remove modules before generation. Choose which sections of your material to include.

Edit every card

Change the words yourself, ask the AI to rewrite a card with an instruction, or draw a new picture. Preview it on a phone frame as you go.

Fact-checked against your source

Each card is checked against the passages it came from. Doubtful cards are flagged with the reason, before any learner sees them.

Pitched at the right level

Reading level is measured on every card and kept to your audience, from children aged 8 to professionals.

Share with one link

Publish a course and send learners one link. No accounts to set up for a quick start.

Export questions

Export the question bank in the QTI standard used by most learning platforms, or export the whole course as a bundle.
