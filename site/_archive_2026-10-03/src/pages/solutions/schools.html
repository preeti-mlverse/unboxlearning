What changes

From "read the chapter" to "show me you get it".

Homework that teaches

Instead of reading pages, students work through a dozen picture-first cards with a game every one or two cards and instant feedback.

Revision that's spaced

The memory deck brings back each idea just before it would be forgotten, so the night before the test isn't the first revision.

Progress by idea

See mastery for every idea in the chapter, not just whether the work was opened. Plan the next lesson around what the class missed.

Does it work for any subject?

Yes. The engine reads what kind of material it has, so a history chapter gets timelines and cause-and-effect games while a science chapter gets process diagrams and ordering games.

Can I check the course before students see it?

Always. You review every card in a phone preview, edit anything, and publish only when you're happy.

Do students need accounts?

Not to start. You share one link. For tracking a whole class over time, we set up the class with you during the pilot.
