Is our material kept private?

Yes. Your documents and courses are visible only to your team and the learners you share them with. We don't use your content to train AI models.

Does it work with our LMS?

Today you can share courses by link and export question banks in QTI. Full SCORM and cmi5 packages for Moodle, TalentLMS, Cornerstone and similar platforms are in development; tell us which platform you use.

What about internal jargon and product names?

The engine works only from your material, so your terms are used as written. You can edit any card, and the fact-check flags anything it can't trace back to your documents.
